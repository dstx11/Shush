# SHUSH Design System Notes

This is a direction document, not a full component library.

## Visual Style
Use:
- dark base
- cold purple and blue accents
- clean typography
- generous spacing
- controlled glow
- premium glass surfaces
- high contrast
- jersey-focused imagery

Avoid:
- childish gamer styling
- constant glitch
- noisy particles
- excessive blur
- cramped sections
- beige/tan or generic SaaS palettes
- generic AI landing-page compositions

## Layout Rules
- Use a consistent max width.
- Keep section spacing deliberate and roomy.
- Keep paragraphs readable; avoid long line lengths.
- Give headings room to breathe.
- Keep CTAs visually attached to the related copy.
- Do not let cards, panels or hero media overlap text on mobile.
- Use grids that collapse cleanly.

## Surface Rules
Glass effects can be used for nav, panels and cards, but they must remain dark and readable.

Use blur carefully:
- nav and small surfaces can use moderate blur
- avoid full-page blur fields
- avoid stacked translucent surfaces that make text muddy

## Jersey Showcase
The jersey is the primary inspectable subject.

The Home and Drop 01 pages should eventually present it with:
- spotlight/depth
- shadow or pedestal feeling
- subtle labels
- premium product framing
- optional pointer tilt on desktop only

Do not make the jersey feel like a thumbnail inside a generic card.

## Header/Nav
Final direction:
- dark Liquid Glass-inspired capsule
- logo left
- nav centered
- Drop 01 CTA right
- clear active state
- strong mobile panel

## Typography
Use compact, confident copy. Headings should be bold and controlled. Body text should stay readable, not tiny or low-contrast.

Avoid exaggerated viewport-scaled typography that causes mobile breakage.
