# SHUSH Copy Guide

## Voice
SHUSH copy should be:
- short
- clean
- confident
- premium
- human
- gamer
- professional
- slightly distinctive

The voice can have personality, but it should not become a roast page.

## Rhythm
Prefer short sentences. Use concrete nouns. Avoid overexplaining.

Good:
- "Sem barulho. Só rounds."
- "Gaming, estética e estrutura. Sem promessas falsas."
- "Uma equipa pequena, uma presença feita com intenção."

Weak:
- "A melhor experiência para todos os apaixonados pelo gaming."
- "Excelência competitiva e união numa comunidade inovadora."
- "Uma presença feita para durar."

## Avoid
- "experiência única"
- "paixão pelo gaming"
- "excelência competitiva"
- "comunidade inovadora"
- "performance e união"
- "elite"
- "world class"
- "legacy"
- excessive jokes
- roasts
- troll language
- AI filler

## Player Copy
Roster text should show personality without humiliating or caricaturing players.

Use:
- role clarity
- concise identity
- controlled humor
- honest descriptions

Avoid:
- insults
- troll labels
- fake stats
- fake achievements
- pretending the roster is a pro league team

## Drop 01 Copy
Frame Drop 01 as the first official/concept jersey piece.

Do:
- be honest that the flow is manual
- mention Backora as part of the visual/technical context
- describe the jersey without inventing production facts

Do not:
- fake scarcity
- fake checkout
- fake stock
- fake material specs
