# SHUSH Brand Guide

## Positioning
SHUSH is a small premium gamer micro-org/team with a strong visual identity.

The brand should be honest about scale:
- not a fake mega organization
- not a meme page
- not a generic corporate team
- not a fake esports history

The strongest SHUSH tone is focused, visual and confident.

## Core Sentence
> Sem barulho. Só rounds.

Use this as the north star for copy, rhythm and interaction. The site should feel controlled, quiet and sharp.

## Brand Attributes
- small but intentional
- dark but readable
- gamer but sophisticated
- professional but not corporate
- interactive but lightweight
- confident but not fake

## Backora
Backora is the technical/digital partner. It must remain integrated with credibility.

Good framing:
- technical/digital partner
- identity/site support
- built with Backora
- first official SHUSH piece created with Backora

Avoid:
- fake sponsor grids
- fake tiered partnerships
- claims that imply a large commercial operation

## Final Pages
- Home
- Roster
- Drop 01
- About

## What Must Disappear
- Call Room as a public concept
- Troll Mode
- meme-heavy/roast copy
- fake esports grandeur
- fake rankings/history/sponsors

## Good Copy Examples
- "Sem barulho. Só rounds."
- "Uma equipa pequena, uma identidade cuidada e uma presença feita para durar."
- "Gaming, estética e estrutura. Sem promessas falsas."
- "A primeira peça oficial da SHUSH, criada com a Backora."
- "SHUSH é uma equipa, uma identidade e um projeto visual em construção."
- "Não somos uma mega organização. Somos uma equipa com direção visual."
