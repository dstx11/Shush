The modified subset uses a new internal family name to respect the Reserved Font
Name. It retains the variable weight axis 400–700, Latin/Latin Extended letters,
Portuguese accents and punctuation. Normal and italic WOFF2, hosted locally.
Original copyright and license records are preserved in the font files.

Manrope and IBM Plex Mono Latin WOFF2 files are unmodified files delivered by
Google Fonts (fonts.gstatic.com) and are now hosted locally. Original SIL OFL
licenses: Manrope-OFL.txt and IBMPlexMono-OFL.txt. Their family names are unchanged.
